Ideas for your surplus

›
Build a buffer first. Aim for 3–6 months of essential costs in an easy-access savings account before investing anything — it's what keeps a car repair or a lost month of income from becoming debt.
Use your ISA allowance. UK residents can shelter up to £20,000 a year in a Cash or Stocks & Shares ISA — interest, dividends and gains inside it are tax-free.
Don't leave employer match on the table. If your workplace pension matches contributions, paying in at least up to that match is close to a guaranteed return before markets even move.
Automate the transfer. Move a fixed amount to savings or investing right after payday, before it has a chance to be spent — pay yourself first, not last.
Clear expensive debt first. Paying down anything charging more than roughly 8–10% interest usually beats what investing is likely to return after costs.

General information for education, not personalised financial advice — for decisions specific to your situation, a regulated financial adviser can weigh your full picture.
